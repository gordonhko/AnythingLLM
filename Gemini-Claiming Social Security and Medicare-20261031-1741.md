## Where to Apply Online for Medicare Part A

To submit your online application, go directly to the official Social Security Administration Medicare portal:
* **[www.ssa.gov/medicare](https://www.ssa.gov/medicare)**

Since you already have a `my Social Security` account and are logged in, you can click on **"Sign up for Medicare"** or **"Start a New Application"** right from your dashboard. When filling it out, you will select the option to sign up for **Medicare only** (leaving your monthly retirement benefit application for later when you turn 67).
